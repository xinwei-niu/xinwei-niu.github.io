I am an MSc graduate in **Artificial Intelligence** from the **University of Edinburgh, School of Informatics**. Previously, I received Bachelor of Science with Honours in **Artificial Intelligence** at the **University of Manchester**. During my undergraduate studies, I interned at the Language Understanding Lab of Samsung Research China, Beijing, gaining hands-on experience with large-scale language technologies and practical AI systems.

My research interests span a broad range of **efficient generative modelling techniques**, including applications in both Natural Language Processing and image and video synthesis. I am particularly interested in **sparse attention methods**, including **hardware-aware sparse attention, KV-cache eviction and compression methods**. Mean while, I am interested in efficient generative architectures ranging from autoregressive and semi-autoregressive models to diffusion-based language models. I also follow advances in emerging state-space model and linear RNN architectures, especially in the context of building scalable, efficient and low-cost generative systems.
